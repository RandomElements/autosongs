Privacy

Local by design — in plain words

AutoSongs has no accounts, no analytics SDK, no ad SDK, and no server of ours in the data path. Everything happens on devices you own. This page spells out exactly what that means, because “trust me” is not a privacy policy.

What the app sees, and where it goes

Data flow of the AutoSongs app
DataWhy it is readWhere it goes
Track title, artist, album, artwork, playback position That is the product: knowing what is playing so it can be saved. Your bridge only — a machine you run. Never to us.
Media notifications Fallback detection for apps that don't expose a full media session. Stay on the phone; only the resulting title/artist pair is sent to your bridge.
Your settings (host, port, token, format, rules) Configuration you chose. Stored locally in the app's own preferences.
The downloaded files The point of it all. Music/AutoSongs/ on your phone, indexed in MediaStore.

When your bridge fetches a track, it talks to YouTube on your behalf — that traffic is between your bridge machine and YouTube, exactly as if you had run yt-dlp yourself. No AutoSongs-operated server is involved in detection, downloading or storage.

Permissions, one by one

Notification access

Android's way of letting an app observe other apps' media sessions and media notifications. Without it there is no detection. You grant it explicitly in system settings, and you can revoke it the same way.

Internet & network state

To talk to your bridge over HTTP — on your phone (Termux) or your local network. The app uses plain HTTP to the bridge you configure, which is why it accepts cleartext traffic on your LAN.

Foreground service & wake lock

Keeps the watcher alive while you listen, with a visible notification. No hidden background process.

Storage (Android 9 and older)

Writing music files on Android 10+ goes through MediaStore and needs no storage permission. On Android 8–9 the app asks for storage access to write Music/AutoSongs/.

Notifications (Android 13+)

For the watcher's own status notification, so you always know it is running.

Battery optimisation exemption

Offered, never forced: without it Android may kill the watcher mid-session.

The bridge, honestly

  • It binds 0.0.0.0 by default so a LAN box works. On any network you don't trust, set --token or keep it behind a firewall.
  • The launcher scripts fetch the bridge program from the AutoSongs project's own server when it is missing or outdated (~/.autosongs/ or %USERPROFILE%\.autosongs\). That download is the only traffic between your machine and us, and it happens only when you run the launcher.
  • Downloads happen through yt-dlp from your bridge machine, so YouTube sees requests from your network — the same as any yt-dlp use.
  • Tokens and pairing codes are shared secrets between your app and your bridge. They never leave that pair.

What does not happen

No collection

No accounts, no profiles, no telemetry, no analytics or ad libraries in the app, no crash reporting to us, no email list. The website itself is static files — it sets no cookies and runs no trackers; your browser simply fetches pages.

No lock-in

Your library is a plain folder of ordinary media files. Delete the app, move the folder, keep the music — nothing is encrypted away or tied to us.

Children and COPPA. AutoSongs collects no personal information from anyone, by design — there is nothing to delete and no data to hand over. It is a general-purpose tool, not a service directed at children.

Third-party software

AutoSongs orchestrates open tools that have their own behaviour and policies: yt-dlp (which contacts YouTube), ffmpeg (encoding), and on Android, Google's MediaStore for library indexing. Their terms and privacy practices apply to their parts of the job. Changes to this page will appear here with the site's next deploy.
